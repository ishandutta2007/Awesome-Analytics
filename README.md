# Awesome-Analytics

# Top Analytics Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Product Analytics, Web Analytics, Event Tracking, Funnels, Session Insights & Privacy-Friendly Measurement*  
**Last updated: September 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Analytics**. These systems capture product and website behavior—events, funnels, retention, session replay, and traffic—so teams can understand users and improve conversion without flying blind.

**Examples** include Mixpanel, Amplitude, Heap, FullStory, Pendo, Google Analytics, PostHog, Kissmetrics, Hotjar, and Contentsquare (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: Analytics has excellent open options. **PostHog**, **Matomo**, **Plausible**, **Umami**, and **Countly** cover product and web analytics with self-hosting. This section is heavily expanded.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Mixpanel, Amplitude, Heap](https://mixpanel.com/)**  
  Leading product analytics platforms—event tracking, funnels, cohorts, retention, and behavioral analysis for product-led teams.

- **[Google Analytics](https://analytics.google.com/)**  
  Ubiquitous web analytics from Google—traffic, acquisition, and conversion reporting for websites and apps.

- **[PostHog (Cloud)](https://posthog.com/)**  
  All-in-one product analytics with session replay, feature flags, experiments, and more—open-source core with managed cloud.

- **[FullStory, Hotjar, Contentsquare, Pendo](https://www.fullstory.com/)**  
  Experience analytics and digital experience platforms—session replay, heatmaps, feedback, and in-app guidance.

- **[Kissmetrics & other classic product analytics](https://www.kissmetrics.io/)**  
  Additional event and revenue-focused analytics tools for SaaS and e-commerce.

- **[Other commercial analytics platforms](https://mixpanel.com/)**  
  Broader BI and customer analytics suites used alongside product analytics.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[PostHog](https://github.com/PostHog/posthog)**  
  Leading open-source (MIT) product analytics platform—events, funnels, session replay, feature flags, experiments, surveys; fully self-hostable.

- **[Matomo](https://github.com/matomo-org/matomo)**  
  Mature open-source (GPL) web analytics—GA-style reports, privacy controls, heatmaps (plugins), and on-prem deployment.

- **[Plausible Analytics](https://github.com/plausible/analytics)**  
  Lightweight, privacy-first open-source (AGPL) web analytics—simple dashboard, cookieless options, easy self-host or cloud.

- **[Umami](https://github.com/umami-software/umami)**  
  Simple, fast open-source (MIT) web analytics—privacy-oriented, easy Docker deploy, events and basic product metrics.

- **[Countly](https://github.com/Countly/countly-server)**  
  Open-source product analytics for web and mobile—self-hosted community edition with push, crash, and engagement features.

- **[GoatCounter, Offen, Rybbit & minimal counters](https://github.com/arp242/goatcounter)**  
  Minimal open web analytics focused on privacy and simplicity.

- **[Ackee, Fathom-adjacent open tools](https://github.com/electerious/Ackee)**  
  Self-hosted analytics with privacy-first designs for smaller sites.

- **[Open telemetry → warehouse stacks](https://github.com/search?q=event+pipeline+OR+analytics+warehouse+open+source)**  
  Event pipelines (e.g. RudderStack open, Snowplow) feeding open BI for custom product analytics.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **Full product analytics**: PostHog as the primary open alternative to Mixpanel/Amplitude.
- **Web analytics / GA replacement**: Matomo, Plausible, or Umami.
- **Mobile + product**: Countly community edition.
- **Composable stacks**: Open SDK → PostHog/Matomo or events → ClickHouse/warehouse → Metabase/Grafana.
- Commercial platforms still lead in polished collaboration, support, and some advanced UX analytics features.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  
**PostHog** for product analytics; **Matomo** / **Plausible** / **Umami** for web traffic.  
Commercial tools (Mixpanel, Amplitude, Heap, GA, FullStory, Hotjar, etc.) provide scale and ecosystem integrations.  
Privacy-conscious and cost-sensitive teams often self-host PostHog or Plausible; growth-stage product teams may prefer commercial product analytics. Fully open stacks are production-ready for most SaaS and website needs.

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- Analytics data is often personal data. Configure consent, retention, and minimization to meet GDPR, CCPA, and local law. Cookieless and self-hosted options reduce vendor risk but do not remove your compliance duties.
- Open-source tools offer data ownership and cost control but require operational effort. Commercial platforms shift infrastructure and support to the vendor. Choose based on privacy posture, feature needs, and team capacity.
